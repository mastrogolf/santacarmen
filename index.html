Monday Guest Guide

How does the All-Inclusive Day Pass work?

When you arrive on Monday, present your digital QR pass at the gate. You will receive your VIP Gold Wristband giving you unlimited access to hitting range balls, equestrian trail rides and horse washing, and ordering whatever you want from the kitchen and bar without ever opening your wallet.

Can I bring my own golf clubs?

Yes, absolutely. However, we also provide professional demo sets at the range station if you prefer to travel light.

What should I wear for the equestrian experiences?

We recommend comfortable closed-toe shoes (boots or sneakers), long pants for riding comfort, and casual resort or western attire.
